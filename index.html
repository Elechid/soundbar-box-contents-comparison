Soundbar box contents comparison

Compare box contents and power requirements for the Samsung HW-Q990H, JBL Bar 800MK2 and Bar 1300MK2.

Plan for four AC outlets with the Q990H. Both JBL models need two, with battery-powered detachable rears.

Samsung HW-Q990H

International HW-Q990H/XT, matched to the store SKU.

Rear speakers
Two wireless rear speakers included. Each requires AC power.
Subwoofer
One wireless subwoofer included.
HDMI cable
Samsung lists an HDMI cable in the manual. Inclusion in the supplied batch remains unverified.
Wall mounting
Main-bar mounting kit included. Rear brackets are absent from the box list.
Power requirements
One AC outlet each for the main bar, subwoofer and both rear speakers, four in total. The XT manual lists four power cords.
Buy separately / supply
Supply suitable rear mounts or stands if needed, choose wall fixings for your wall type, and supply an HDMI cable if the box lacks one.
Unverified
Unverified: HDMI cable inclusion, plug type and each component’s voltage label for the store batch.

JBL Bar 800MK2

Listed as international stock. Regional SKU and plug type are unverified.

Rear speakers
Two detachable rechargeable rear speakers included.
Subwoofer
One 10-inch wireless subwoofer included.
HDMI cable
JBL lists one 1.2 m HDMI cable.
Wall mounting
JBL lists main-bar and rear-speaker brackets, screws and a mounting template.
Power requirements
The main bar and sub each need AC power. The rears run on rechargeable batteries and dock on the bar to charge. JBL specifies 100–240 V AC, 50/60 Hz.
Buy separately / supply
For separate charging, supply a USB-C cable and compatible power source.
Unverified
Unverified: the regional SKU, power-cord count and plug type for the store batch. JBL says these vary by region.
